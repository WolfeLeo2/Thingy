Privacy Policy

Thingy

Effective 26 July 2026 muneneboizkaruga@gmail.com

In short

Thingy stores what you save (photos, videos, notes, links) and uses Google Gemini to tag and organize it automatically.

Photos keep their location if the photo has it — that's what powers the map.

Your media is hosted on Cloudinary, a third-party CDN, so it works across your devices.

If you use "Find shopping links," a short text query is sent to SerpAPI.

No ads, no analytics, nothing sold to anyone.

You can delete your account in Settings — full erase after 30 days, undo any time before that.

01What we collect

Thingy is a personal save-it-for-later app. Here's what it stores, and why.

Account

You sign in with Google. We receive your email address, display name, profile photo URL, and a unique account id from Google — nothing else from your Google account.

What you save

Photos, videos, notes, and links you save, plus whatever Thingy derives from them: tags, a short description, and (for photos and videos with location data) the coordinates embedded in the file. That's what puts a saved photo on the map — Thingy doesn't track your location any other way, and never accesses it outside of what's already in a file you chose to save.

On-device only, never sent anywhere

If you turn on Smart Search, a small AI model is downloaded and runs entirely on your phone. It reads what you've saved to make search understand meaning, not just keywords — none of that ever leaves your device.

02Who else touches your data

Thingy is built on a few outside services. Here's exactly what each one sees and why it's there.

ServiceWhat it receivesWhy
Google Firebase Account info, saved-item metadata, shared-space membership Sign-in and the app's database

None of these companies are paid to advertise to you through Thingy, and Thingy doesn't sell or rent your data to anyone, for any reason.

03Shared spaces

Thingy lets you share a "space" with friends by invite code. Anything you add to a shared space becomes visible to everyone else in that space — that's the point of sharing it. Leaving a space (or deleting your account) removes your access and revokes anything you'd shared into it.

04How long we keep things

As long as your account exists, your data exists — there's no automatic expiry on an active account. If you delete an item or leave a space, that change happens immediately.

05Deleting your account

Settings → Delete account. Here's exactly what happens:

The 30-day window

  1. You're signed out immediately.
  2. For 30 days, your data is untouched — sign back in any time in that window and you'll be offered the choice to cancel and keep your account exactly as it was.
  3. After 30 days, everything is permanently deleted: your saved items, shared-space memberships, media on Cloudinary, and your account itself. This can't be undone once it runs.

If you owned a space shared with friends, that space is deleted along with your account rather than handed to someone else — your friends keep whatever they'd saved outside of it, but the shared space itself goes away.

06Security

Sign-in goes through Google, not a password we manage. Access to your saved items and shared spaces is enforced by Firebase's security rules on every request, not just checked in the app.

07Children

Thingy isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone under that age.

08Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the "Effective" date at the top will change and, where reasonable, we'll let you know in the app.
